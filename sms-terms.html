SMS Notifications

Digital Double Technologies Inc ("DDT") may send SMS notifications to creators who have provided a phone number and opted in to alerts. These notifications include:

  • Unauthorized access attempt alerts on your consent records
  • Consent record status changes (revocation, suspension)
  • Verification codes for account authentication

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on consent record activity. You will typically receive no more than 5 messages per month. Critical security alerts (unauthorized access attempts) are sent immediately regardless of frequency.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. DDT does not charge for SMS notifications. Your carrier's standard messaging rates apply.

Opt Out

Reply STOP to any DDT SMS message to opt out of all SMS notifications. You may also remove your phone number from your account settings in the DDT creator portal. Opting out of SMS does not affect email notifications or your consent records.

Help

Reply HELP to any DDT SMS message for assistance, or contact jhofstad@ddt-pro.com.

Carrier Disclaimer

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. DDT consent record protections operate independently of SMS delivery. Your consent records remain protected whether or not SMS notifications are received.
