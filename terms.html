Program

Digital Double Technologies Consent Notification Service.

Description of service

Digital Double Technologies provides consent infrastructure for creators. When you create a Consent Receipt Object (CRO) or accept a collaboration invitation, the service sends notifications by email and SMS to keep you informed about activity tied to your consent records. Notifications include access alerts when a third party queries one of your assets, collaboration invitations, re-authorization requests, and revocation events.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your account activity. You may receive zero messages in a quiet week and several in a day if your assets are being actively queried. You will only receive messages tied to your own consent records or invitations addressed to you.

Message and data rates

Standard message and data rates may apply. Charges are determined by your mobile carrier and are not controlled by Digital Double Technologies.

Opt-out

Reply STOP to any SMS message to opt out of further messages. You will receive a single confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent to that number.

Help

Reply HELP to any SMS message to receive support information. For all other questions, contact jhofstad@ddt-pro.com.
